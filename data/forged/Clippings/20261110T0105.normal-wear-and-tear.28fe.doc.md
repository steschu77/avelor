**Normal wear and tear: what landlords can't charge for**

A landlord may keep part of a security deposit for damage, but not for normal wear and tear.

Normal wear and tear includes:

- small nail holes from hanging pictures
- light bulbs that burned out
- faded paint and worn carpet in walking areas

Damage includes large holes, broken windows, and stains. If your landlord deducts for wear and tear, dispute it in writing within 14 days.
