**How to spot a real Shelby GT500**

1. Every real 1967 Shelby GT500 has a Shelby serial number, starting with 67400, on top of its Ford VIN.
2. The engine is the 428, not the 289.
3. The Ford VIN of a Shelby starts with 7R02S, not 7F02C.
4. If the seller says "trust me", it is not a Shelby.
